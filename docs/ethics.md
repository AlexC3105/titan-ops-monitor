# TITAN — Ethics

TITAN may eventually touch disasters, public safety, economics, and human behavior. That makes
ethics a **design constraint**, not an afterthought.

## Core rule

> **TITAN must present probabilistic analysis, not deterministic prophecy.**

Every outcome ships with a probability, a confidence level, and explicit data gaps. The product is
forbidden from claiming certainty.

## Hard guardrails (non-negotiable)

1. **No individuals.** Model groups, cohorts, and systems — never real, identifiable people.
2. **No sensitive personal data.** No tracking, profiling, or re-identification. Population data is
   aggregate only.
3. **No false certainty.** No "this will happen." Outcomes are likelihoods with confidence.
4. **No operational/tactical military or conflict use.** TITAN is not a targeting or C2 tool and
   must not be framed as one.
5. **Civilian-first scope.** Start with weather, traffic, infrastructure, utilities, transportation,
   supply chain, and emergency planning.
6. **Transparency.** Always show drivers, assumptions, and data gaps. No black-box verdicts.
7. **Honest limitations.** Clearly label unvalidated/heuristic outputs as such.

## Why these matter

- **Disaster contexts amplify harm.** A confident-but-wrong evacuation or fuel prediction can hurt
  people. Probabilistic framing + visible uncertainty keeps humans in the decision loop.
- **Aggregation protects dignity.** Group/system modeling captures useful dynamics without
  surveilling anyone.
- **Scope discipline prevents mission creep.** The civilian framing is a deliberate boundary.

## Responsible-use expectations

TITAN supports **planning and understanding**, not automated decisions. Outputs are decision
*support* for humans, who remain accountable. High-stakes actions should corroborate TITAN with
authoritative sources (e.g. official NWS/NHC advisories, local emergency management).

## Review

Ethics is revisited before any new data source, scenario type, or
region is added. New capabilities are checked against the hard guardrails above before shipping.
